# 🚀 Job Genie - AI-Powered LMS & Career Development Platform

## 📌 Overview

**Job Genie** is an AI-powered Learning Management System (LMS) and Career Development SaaS Platform designed to help students, fresh graduates, and professionals become job-ready and accelerate their career growth.

The platform combines resume building, interview preparation, career guidance, personalized learning, and job recommendations into a single intelligent ecosystem powered by AI.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 AI Resume Builder

* Create professional and ATS-friendly resumes.
* Multiple resume templates.
* Easy editing and customization.

### 📝 AI Cover Letter Generator

* Generate personalized cover letters instantly.
* Tailored to specific job roles and industries.

### 🎯 ATS Score Analysis & Resume Optimization

* Analyze resumes against ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).
* Get optimization suggestions to improve resume visibility.

### 💼 Personalized Job Recommendations

* AI-driven job suggestions based on:

  * Skills
  * Experience
  * Career interests
  * Resume content

### 📚 Skill-Based Course Recommendations

* Discover relevant learning resources.
* Bridge skill gaps required for target job roles.

### 🛣️ AI Career Roadmaps

* Generate personalized career growth plans.
* Step-by-step guidance for achieving career goals.

### 🎤 AI Voice Interview Simulator

* Practice interviews with AI-generated questions.
* Real-time feedback on responses.
* Improve communication and interview performance.

### 💬 AI Career Assistant Chatbot

* Get instant career guidance.
* Ask questions related to jobs, skills, resumes, and career planning.

### 📊 User Dashboard

Track everything in one place:

* Resumes
* ATS Scores
* Job Applications
* Learning Progress
* Career Roadmaps

### 📈 Analytics & Performance Tracking

* Interactive charts and graphs.
* Monitor career growth and platform engagement.

### 👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard

* User management
* Activity monitoring
* Analytics dashboard
* Platform administration
